## Bio

Jon Myers is a Research Scientist in the Human-AI Resonance Lab. Prior to coming
to MIT, he served as a researcher in systematic musicology at UC Santa Cruz, where
he created the
[IDTAP](https://swara.studio), an NEH-funded, web-based platform with a custom
notation and data representation for the transcription and analysis of Hindustani
music.

He received his DMA in algorithmic music composition from UC Santa Cruz in 2022,
following a BA in Music from Wesleyan University and an MA in Music Composition
from Mills College. His broader work spans generative composition, spatial audio
and ambisonic field recording, and the computational modeling of world music
traditions.

His current research centers on representation and epistemic bias in music AI —
designing data architectures that engage diverse musical traditions on their own
analytical terms — as well as speculative music theory, principally around just
intonation and non-metric temporality.
